Adventures in Fire Island

Trip Highlights

My recent escape to Fire Island was an unforgettable journey through pristine maritime nature and serene coastal shores. Walking through the Sunken Forest, surrounded by rare 300-year-old holly trees tucked behind the dunes, offered a peaceful contrast to the Atlantic surf.

From spotting native wildlife along the salt marshes to watching breathtaking sunsets at the historic Fire Island Lighthouse, the absence of cars made the entire trip feel like stepping into an untouched coastal sanctuary.
